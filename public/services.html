Process

How a project runs

The same four stages, whether it is a single photo day or a month of content.

01

Brief

We learn what the business does, who it speaks to and what the work needs to achieve.

02

Plan

Concept, script or shot list, locations and a schedule you sign off before anything is filmed.

03

Produce

Our crew shoots photo and video on location or in studio, lit and framed for the brand.

04

Deliver

Editing, colour and sound, then files cut for each platform, with a round of revisions.
